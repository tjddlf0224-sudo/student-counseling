Counseling Log

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026

Counseling Log ("the app") is built for teachers, counselors, and advisors to record and organize conversations with students. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. The app is for use by adults managing their own professional records — it is not directed at children, and children are not expected to use it directly.

Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google, Apple, or email/password, we receive your email address and a unique account ID from that provider. This identifies your account and keeps your data separate from every other user's.

Content you create. The student roster and counseling records you enter — names, ID numbers, dates, notes, tags, and any other details you type — are stored under your account so you can retrieve and organize them later. This content is entered and controlled entirely by you; we do not read, review, or use it for any purpose other than displaying it back to you.

Advertising identifier. The app shows ads (banner and optional rewarded video) through Google AdMob to help keep the app free. AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and related technical data to serve and measure ads, under Google's own privacy policy. We do not personally identify you through advertising, and ad data is never linked to your counseling records.

Notification preference. If you turn on weekday reminders, the on/off setting and reminder time are stored locally on your device only. They are not sent to our servers.

Unsaved drafts (kept on your device). While you are writing a counseling entry, what you have typed is saved temporarily on the device you are using, so it is not lost if the app closes or you navigate away. This draft stays on that device only — it is never sent to our servers, and it is kept separately for each signed-in account, so another person signing in on the same device cannot see it. The draft is cleared as soon as the entry is saved, when you discard it, or when you delete your account. Because it is stored on the device, we recommend using the app on a device only you can unlock.

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and keep your records private to your account
  • To store, sync, and display your student and counseling records across your own devices
  • To generate the PDF exports and reports you request (the Korean-language version of the app also offers an Excel export formatted for Korea's NEIS school system)
  • To operate ad-supported free access to the app
  • To send you the optional local reminder notification, if enabled

We do not sell your information, and we do not use your counseling content for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond providing the app to you.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your account and content are stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Data is scoped per account — only you, signed into your own account, can read or write your records. We do not manually access individual users' counseling records except as needed to investigate a reported problem or as required by law.

Third-Party Services

The app relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase — authentication and data storage
  • Google AdMob — advertising

Your Choices

Export. You can export your own records at any time as a PDF file directly from the app.

Delete your account. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from within the app:

  1. Open Settings in the app.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm your identity (re-sign-in may be required).
  4. Your account and all student/counseling records are permanently deleted.

This is processed immediately and cannot be undone — no separate email request is needed. You can also email us to request deletion.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for use by adult professionals (teachers, counselors, advisors) and is not directed at children. It is not designed for direct use by children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. Student names and notes entered by a user are professional records controlled by that user, not information collected from a child interacting with the app.

Data Retention

Your content is retained until you delete it or delete your account. Deleting your account removes your account information and all associated records permanently.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
