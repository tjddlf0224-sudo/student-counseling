Counseling Log

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Counseling Log ("the app") is built for teachers, counselors, and advisors to record and organize conversations with students. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. The app is for use by adults managing their own professional records — it is not directed at children, and children are not expected to use it directly.

A personal notebook, not an official school record system. Counseling Log is a personal tool for your own working notes. It is not a school's official student record or case-management system and is not approved by any school, school board, or education department. Before recording information about students, please check your school's or employer's policy on where student information may be kept, and keep what you enter to the minimum you need — for example, use initials or your own internal numbers instead of full names, and never enter government-issued student or health identification numbers. You are responsible for having the authority (for example from your school, or the consent required by your local law) to record information about students, including students under 18.

Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google, Apple, or email/password, we receive your email address and a unique account ID from that provider. This identifies your account and keeps your data separate from every other user's.

Content you create. The student roster and counseling records you enter — names, ID numbers, dates, notes, tags, and any other details you type — are stored under your account so you can retrieve and organize them later. This content is entered and controlled entirely by you; we do not read, review, or use it for any purpose other than displaying it back to you.

Advertising identifier. The app shows ads (banner and optional rewarded video) through Google AdMob to help keep the app free. With your permission, AdMob collects your device's advertising identifier (such as IDFA on iOS or GAID on Android) and related technical data to serve personalized ads and measure ad performance, under Google's own privacy policy. If you decline or your device does not provide an advertising identifier, you will see non-personalized ads instead. We do not personally identify you through advertising, and ad data is never linked to your counseling records. In the English version, ads are non-personalized: they are not based on your past behavior, though Google may still use limited device information for frequency capping, fraud prevention and aggregated measurement.

Usage statistics and usage records. To understand how the app is actually used and where to improve it, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and our own Firebase project to record anonymous usage: which screens you open, when the app is opened or moved to the background, how many times you save a record, add a student, or run an export, and whether an ad was offered, shown, completed, or closed. Each usage record contains only the event name, simple values such as a screen name or a number of seconds, the time, the platform (iOS, Android, or web), and the app version — the text of your counseling records, student names, and student ID numbers are never included. Usage records are tied to a randomly generated number created on your device. This number is not linked to your account, your email address, your advertising identifier, or any student, and we do not use it to identify you. Usage records are deleted automatically after 13 months.

Install source statistics. When the Android app is launched for the first time, it records once to Supabase how the app was installed (e.g., Instagram, a blog, Google Play search): the source name, app version, and date. It contains no information that can identify your device or account and is used only to count installs per source.

Technical diagnostics. To find and fix problems such as slow or failed saves, the app keeps a small daily diagnostic record under your account: the platform and browser type, app version, how long saving and loading took, and short error codes. It never contains the text of your records or any student information.

Notification preference. If you turn on weekday reminders, the on/off setting and reminder time are stored locally on your device only. They are not sent to our servers.

Unsaved drafts (kept on your device). While you are writing or editing a counseling entry, what you have typed is saved temporarily on the device you are using, so it is not lost if the app closes or you navigate away. This applies both to new entries and to edits of existing entries; edit drafts are kept per record, and the app keeps only the most recent ones (currently up to 20 per account), removing the oldest beyond that. A draft stays on that device only — it is never sent to our servers, and it is kept separately for each signed-in account, so another person signing in on the same device cannot see it. A draft is cleared once the entry is saved, when you discard it, or when you delete your account. One exception: if a save could not be confirmed (for example you were offline), the draft is deliberately kept until that save is resolved, so your text is not lost. Because drafts are stored on the device, we recommend using the app on a device only you can unlock.

Other data kept on your device. To make the app easier to use, a few small items are stored locally and never sent to our servers: the tags you recently used when searching, your theme and language preferences, and — if a student ID change was interrupted before it finished — a short note of that student's ID and name so the app can complete the change next time. These are stored per signed-in account and are removed when you delete your account.

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and keep your records private to your account
  • To store, sync, and display your student and counseling records across your own devices
  • To generate the PDF exports and reports you request
  • To operate ad-supported free access to the app
  • To send you the optional local reminder notification, if enabled
  • To measure anonymous usage (counts and usage records) so we can improve the app

We do not sell your information, and we do not use your counseling content for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond providing the app to you.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your student roster, counseling records, settings, technical diagnostics, and anonymous usage records are stored in Google Cloud Firestore in South Korea (Google Cloud Seoul region). Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, and usage statistics and advertising by Google Analytics for Firebase and Google AdMob; Google may process that information in the United States and other countries where it operates. Install source totals (Android) are stored with Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd.) on servers in South Korea (Seoul). Data is scoped per account — only you, signed into your own account, can read or write your records. We do not manually access individual users' counseling records except as needed to investigate a reported problem or as required by law.

International Transfers

The app is operated from South Korea. If you use it from another country, the information described above is transferred to and stored in South Korea, and some of it (sign-in, usage statistics, advertising) is processed by Google in the United States and other countries. These countries' privacy laws may differ from those where you live. Google (for Cloud Firestore and sign-in) and Supabase store and process this information on our behalf, under their data processing terms and security programs. Google AdMob and Google Analytics for Firebase are different: Google processes that information under its own privacy policy, for the purposes described in that policy. Please consider this, together with your school's policy, before entering information about students.

Security and Data Breaches

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by account-based access rules so that one account cannot read another's records. You can also turn on the app lock (Face ID or fingerprint). No system is perfectly secure; if we become aware of a breach involving your personal information that is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify affected users and, where required, the relevant privacy regulator, as required by applicable law.

Third-Party Services

The app relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase — authentication and data storage
  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Supabase — install source totals (Android, first launch only; no device or account identifiers)
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — anonymous usage statistics and usage events (which screens and features are used, how often, and how ads are used). Google Analytics keeps this data for up to 14 months. Your counseling records and student information are never sent to this service.

Your Choices

Export. You can export your own records at any time as a PDF file directly from the app.

Ad tracking. On iOS, the app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before showing personalized ads. You can allow or deny this in the system prompt, or change your choice later in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, you can reset or limit your advertising ID in device Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Delete your account. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from within the app:

  1. Open Settings in the app.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm your identity (re-sign-in may be required).
  4. Your account and all student/counseling records are permanently deleted.

Your records are removed from the app immediately and this cannot be undone — no separate email request is needed. Copies in Google's systems are then erased on Google's schedule, within a maximum of about 180 days under its data processing terms. You can also email us to request deletion. If your school requires counseling notes to be kept for a period of time, export them before deleting your account.

Access and correction. You can view, edit, and delete your own records in the app at any time. To ask what personal information we hold about you, or to have it corrected, email us at the address below.

It is not designed for direct use by children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. Student names and notes entered by a user are professional records controlled by that user, not information collected from a child interacting with the app.

Data Retention

Anonymous usage records are not linked to your account and are deleted automatically after 13 months; Google Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. Technical diagnostics are deleted together with your account.
